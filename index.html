A Letter For You

✦

Happy birthday, my love. I don't think words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me.

You are not only the person I love, but also my best friend, my comfort, my happiness, and the person I want beside me through every part of life.

Thank you for every laugh, every memory, every late conversation, and every small moment that somehow became unforgettable because it was with you.

I hope this year gives you everything you deserve and even more.

I love you. Always. ♡
